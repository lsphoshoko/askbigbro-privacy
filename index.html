Ask Big Bro ("the app," "we," "us," or "our") is a social confidence training application. This privacy policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

Information We Collect

Account information. When you create an account, we collect your email address. This is used to sign you in and to identify your account across devices.

Training data. We store the following information on our servers, linked to your account:

  • Your progress through the training levels
  • Your mission logs, including the date, result, feel rating, and any notes you write
  • Your streak and completion statistics
  • Your assessment answers (used only to place you at the correct starting level)

Device information. Our authentication and database provider (Google Firebase) may collect limited technical information necessary to operate the service, such as device type and app version. We do not collect advertising identifiers, location data, contacts, photos, or any other device data.

We do not collect: your name (unless you choose to enter it), your phone number, your contacts, your location, photos, camera or microphone data, or advertising identifiers.

How We Use Your Data

We use your data only to operate the app:

  • To authenticate you and keep you signed in
  • To save and sync your training progress across your devices
  • To display your mission, scoreboard, history, and analytics
  • To calculate your streaks and completion rates

We do not use your data for advertising. We do not sell your data. We do not share your data with third parties for their own purposes.

Third-Party Services

We use Google Firebase for authentication and data storage. Firebase processes your email address and training data on our behalf. Firebase's privacy practices are governed by Google's Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

We do not use any other third-party services that collect personal data.

Data Sharing

We do not share your personal data with any third party except Google Firebase, which acts as our service provider and processes data only as instructed by us.

Data Retention and Deletion

Your data is retained for as long as your account exists.

To delete your account and all associated data: Email us at info@pinvoyage.co.za with the subject "Delete my account." We will delete your account and all associated training data within 30 days.

If you uninstall the app, your account and data remain on our servers until you request deletion. Uninstalling does not delete your account.

Security

Your data is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS) and stored in Google Firebase, which applies industry-standard security practices. We do not store passwords in plain text; authentication is handled by Firebase.

Children's Privacy

Ask Big Bro is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

Your Rights

You have the right to: access the data we hold about you, correct inaccurate data, delete your account and data, and export your data (contact us to request an export). To exercise any of these rights, email [your email address].

Changes to This Policy

If we change how we handle data, this policy will be updated before those changes take effect. The updated policy will be posted at this URL with a new "Last updated" date.
